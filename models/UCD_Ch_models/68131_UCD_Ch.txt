@startuml
title "Cheers System Use Case Diagram"

' General Employee Actor
actor "Employee" as Employee
actor "Sales Employee" as SalesEmployee
actor "Marketing Employee" as MarketingEmployee
actor "Gefen System" as Gefen
actor "Customer" as Customer

' Employee can also act as Customer
Employee <|-- Customer : "can act as"

' Inheritance Relationships
Employee <|-- SalesEmployee
Employee <|-- MarketingEmployee

' Employee Operations
Employee --> (View System Data)

' Customer Operations
Customer --> (Place Joint Order)
Customer --> (Make Order)

' Sales Employee Operations
SalesEmployee --> (Place Customer Order)
(Place Customer Order) <|-- (Place Urgent Order) : extends
(Place Customer Order) <|-- (Place Regular Order) : includes
(Place Regular Order) <|-- (Place Joint Order) : extends

SalesEmployee --> (Browse Available Wines)

SalesEmployee --> (Generate Reports)
(Generate Reports) <|-- (Generate Unproductive Employees Report) : extends
(Generate Reports) <|-- (Generate Wine Recommendation Report) : extends

' Marketing Employee Operations
MarketingEmployee --> (Record Customer Details)
MarketingEmployee --> (Recommend Occasions and Provide Description)

MarketingEmployee --> (Link Wines to Types)
(Link Wines to Types) <|-- (Modify Price Details and Product Images) : extends

MarketingEmployee --> (Update Wine Information and Types)

' Gefen System Operations
Gefen --> (Send Monthly XML Data)
(Send Monthly XML Data) <|-- (Include Wine Producers and Wines) : includes

Gefen --> (Send Weekly Quantity Updates)

' Notes for clarity
note right of (Place Urgent Order) : Urgent orders have priority\nand require a single customer.
note right of (Place Joint Order) : Joint orders optimize delivery costs.\nPlaced by customers or employees acting as customers.
@enduml
