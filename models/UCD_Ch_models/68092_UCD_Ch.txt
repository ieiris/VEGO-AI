' Extension point notes
note right of RegularOrder
**Extension Point: Joint Order Management**
Triggered when multiple customers place a combined 
order to reduce delivery costs. The system tracks 
wine selections for each customer and designates 
a main customer for the order.
end note

note right of Order
**Extension Point:**
Urgent Order Handling: Triggered when an order is marked as urgent, 
requiring prioritization and urgency level. 
Regular Order: Triggered when the order is not urgent, 
allowing multiple customers to combine their orders to reduce delivery costs.
end note
